AquaMath

Honest aquarium math. Real volume after substrate, heater watts that match your room, filter turnover that matches your fish, and stocking by adult size.

The box number is a lie. Substrate and hardscape displace about a tenth of the advertised volume. Every downstream number - heater, filter, stocking, water changes - runs off the real gallons, not the sticker.
Equipment sized by physics. 3-5W per gallon depending on how cold your room runs, rounded up to a heater that actually exists. Filter turnover at 4-5x for community tanks, 7x for messy fish. Gravel by footprint and depth, not by the bag's optimism.
Stocking without the pet-store fiction. The inch-per-gallon rule, kept honest: slim fish only, adult inches only, half credit for tall-bodied fish, and a hard no to goldfish math (20 gal for the first, +10 each). The 2-inch oscar becomes a 12-inch problem.
